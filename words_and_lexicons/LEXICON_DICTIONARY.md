## 2. Combinatoric Formula: The Physical Prompt Engine

High-yield prompts are generated by assembling words across layers rather than writing generic prose:

$$\text{Prompt} = [\text{Material / Color}] + [\text{Being / Subject}] + [\text{Distortion / Physical Event}] + [\text{Object / Hardware}] + [\text{Environment}] + [\text{Photo / Finish}]$$

### Example Generation:
> *"Glossy fiberglass humanoid with head turned 180 degrees backwards and exposed chrome ribs, clutching a warped dental rig clamped with red scissors, standing on bare concrete in a cavernous brutalist gallery, Gagosian-style soft diffuse overhead light, institutional catalog photograph."*
