# Alex Stream-of-Consciousness Prompts (Emergency Love / Roadside Weather)
Emergency love on the highway: canned vegetables and flashlight beams cutting through amber ambulance lights, billboards reflecting moving cars across wet asphalt.
People of all ages doing high-velocity cartwheels across a suburban Baltimore highway with long arms stretching into traffic, speed limit 55.
A car running on the highway with long flexible human arms instead of wheels, switching lanes at high velocity past streetlights, sulphur yellow haze.
Tigers and a large multi-wheeled truck under highway streetlights, hands tightly gripping the wheel, Buena Vista tortilla texture on the sky.
High velocity getaway car with red emergency light, open expansion joints on a concrete bridge, rain-soaked summer roadside.
