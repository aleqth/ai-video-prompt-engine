# SCARY VEE: THE MULTIVERSE OF HUSTLE — SERIES BIBLE

## 1. Core Lore & Creative Premise
In every dimension of the multiverse, Gary Vaynerchuk was infected, re-engineered, or cursed by an eldritch supernatural force—yet his primal instinct to **flip, hustle, arbitrage, and call out excuses** remains 1000% intact. He roams mundane, real-world everyday settings (supermarkets, suburban garage sales, corporate elevators, laundromats) confronting ordinary people with unhinged, viral motivational aggression.

---

## 2. Voice & Linguistic DNA
- **Authentic Pacing & Cadence:** Rapid-fire East Coast conversational bursts, sudden blunt rhetorical questions (*"Are you out of your mind?!"*), and deep personal eye contact (*"Look at me."*).
- **Core Tropes & Keywords:** Macro patience, micro speed, garage sales, Aisle 4 arbitrage, eBay listings, vintage cereal boxes, attention currency, blaming the algorithm.
- **Trained Vocal Model (`gary_vee_master_voice_model.json`):**
  - **Dataset:** 8 diverse high-definition reference stems (599.4 seconds of clean speech across keynotes, podcasts, garage sales, and Q&As in `gary_vee_voice_dataset/references/`).
  - **Formant Resonances:** Signature 2.85 kHz nasal rasp, 1.35 kHz chest warmth, and 480 Hz vocal fry.
